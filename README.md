# Aarohan Bank – ServiceNow Cybersecurity GRC Portfolio Project

## Project Overview
A self-directed Cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) project developed using a ServiceNow Developer Instance for a fictional banking organization, Aarohan Bank.

The project demonstrates practical understanding of cybersecurity risk identification, risk assessment, risk prioritization, control assessment, and remediation documentation.

## 1. Risk Assessment & Register Development
- Created a custom Cybersecurity Risk Register table in ServiceNow.
- Configured fields to capture Risk ID, Risk Title, Description, Likelihood, and Impact.
- Documented 10 simulated cybersecurity risks relevant to a banking environment.
- Applied a 5×5 risk assessment methodology, scoring likelihood and impact on a scale of 1–5.
- Calculated risk scores and categorized risks into Low, Medium, High, and Critical severity levels.
- Documented risk ownership and recommended mitigation activities.

## 2. Control Testing & Assessment – BR-001
**Risk:** Unauthorized Exposure of Customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

**Control:** Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

- Documented a simulated MFA control assessment using a sample of 10 user accounts.
- Identified that 2 out of 10 sample accounts did not have MFA enabled.
- Recorded the control test outcome as Failed.
- Assigned a likelihood score of 4 and impact score of 5.
- Calculated a risk score of 20 (Critical).
- Identified evidence requirements, including MFA configuration settings, authentication logs, and access review records.

## 3. Remediation & Risk Mitigation
- Recommended enabling MFA across all applicable user accounts.
- Proposed investigation and remediation of accounts without MFA.
- Documented remediation ownership and follow-up activities.
- Recommended evidence validation and control retesting after remediation.

## 4. Framework References
Used selected concepts and references from:
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0.
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
- Risk assessment, access control, security monitoring, and remediation practices.

## 5. Tools & Deliverables
- **ServiceNow Developer Instance:** Custom Cybersecurity Risk Register and 10 simulated risk records.
- **GitHub:** Project documentation, README, assessment summary, and screenshots.
- **Risk Assessment Methodology:** 5×5 Likelihood and Impact scoring model.

## Project Outcome
Developed practical familiarity with documenting cybersecurity risks, prioritizing risk exposure, assessing security controls, identifying control gaps, and recommending remediation actions within a GRC workflow.

**Disclaimer:** Aarohan Bank is a fictional organization. All risk records, account samples, control assessment findings, and remediation scenarios are simulated for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes. No production banking systems or real customer information were accessed.
